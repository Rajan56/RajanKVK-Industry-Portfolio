Is the process still in control?

Forty measurements of a thickness target of 50.0 mm. Introduce a shift or more spread after sample 20 and watch the control chart, the capability index and the significance test react.

Six Sigma methods

Welch t
p value

Control limits come from the first 20 samples using the average moving range. Signals: a point beyond three sigma, or eight in a row on one side of the centre line. Specification limits 46.0 to 54.0 mm. The test compares samples 1 to 20 with 21 to 40.

How I work

Start on the floor, finish with a number someone owns

Understand the work

I walk the process and talk to the people who run it before opening any dataset. Two years as a supervisor taught me where the real story sits.

Measure and model

Data is pulled into one model. KPIs are defined so that finance, operations and sustainability read the same figure.

Test with users

A prototype dashboard or twin goes to the people who will use it. What they ignore gets removed.

Hand over and prove

Every recommendation has an owner, a baseline and a way to check the benefit afterwards.

About

I help industrial companies find where performance is won and lost, and act on it

Most plants collect far more data than they use. Margins slip, energy bills climb and sustainability targets tighten, while the figures that would explain why sit in separate systems and departments. My work is to connect them, show what truly drives the result, and hand managers a clear answer they can act on.

Two kinds of experience meet in that work. I have led daily production in a manufacturing plant, answerable for output, quality and people. And as a Doctor of Science in Industrial Engineering and Management from LUT University, I spent four years with European industrial partners studying how AI, IoT and digital twins change the way companies measure and manage performance.

What your company gains

  • A clear view of what drives profitWhich products, customers and process conditions earn money and which quietly cost it, shown in one model that finance and operations both trust.
  • Lower cost and less wasteBottlenecks, scrap and energy losses located, valued in euros and ranked, so effort goes where the return is largest.
  • Investments you can defendThe true benefit of a digital or energy project measured with sound statistics, before the next one is approved.
  • Sustainability figures that hold upEnergy and CO₂ indicators tied to daily operations and ready for reporting, customers and auditors.
  • Technology that people useDigital twins, dashboards and AI applications designed around the decisions your team makes every day.
